# Artist broadening batch 54 — source audit

Checked: 2026-10-09. Six complete records were selected after exact and normalized-name searches across the aggregate artist catalog. Sally Mann and Christian Marclay appeared only in shortlist/supplementary material, not as complete `Artist` records. Four further artists were selected from the Prix Pictet 2019 Hope shortlist. Awoiska van der Molen and Joana Choumali were excluded because complete records already exist.

## Coverage
- American Southern landscape, wildfire and the racial memory of the Great Dismal Swamp
- American-Swiss sampling practice connecting sound, collage, animation and mass-media fire imagery
- Namibian long-term social landscape work on drought, migration and postcolonial history
- Azerbaijani documentary work on Transylvanian hay-meadow knowledge and agrarian change
- American plant-based landscape practice treating visual connection as an ecological and social form
- South African participatory street studios that return free family portraits to communities
