# Artist broadening batch 56 — source audit

Checked: 2026-10-09. Six complete records were selected from the Prix Pictet 2017 Space shortlist after exact and normalized-name searches across the aggregate catalog. Richard Mosse, Rinko Kawauchi, Thomas Ruff and Michael Wolf were excluded because complete records already exist. Pavel Wolberg and Sergey Ponomarev remain candidates for a later batch.

## Coverage
- British experimental photography that turns collected marine plastic into pseudo-plankton specimens
- German photographic, botanical and living archives of office plants
- German computer-composited fictional cities critiquing modernist ideology
- Hong Kong high-angle interiors documenting subdivided flats and housing inequality
- Bangladeshi black-and-white landscape work on borders, extraction and territorial identity
- Japanese walking, dense shooting and hand-collage as subjective urban cartography
