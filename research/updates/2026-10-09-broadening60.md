# Broadening batch 60 — Prix Pictet Power audit

Date: 2026-10-09

## Scope

- Added six previously missing complete records from the Prix Pictet **Power** shortlist: Luc Delahaye, Daniel Beltrá, Philippe Chancel, Edmund Clark, Jacqueline Hassink and Guy Tillim.
