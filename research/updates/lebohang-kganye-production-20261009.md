# Lebohang Kganye — production audit (2026-10-09)

The artist's work *Mosebetsi wa Dirithi* uses cotton twill patchwork on organza. The artist's catalogue gives *Gladys* (2022) as 275 × 101 cm, whereas a gallery lists 266 × 101 cm. These measurements must not be conflated. Camera, cutting, and sewing equipment are not confirmed.
