# A Cup of Dirt — critical image inventory (2026-10-10)

Artist: Sheung Yiu. Primary source: https://www.sheungyiu.com/a-cup-of-dirt-1

The artist publishes 15 image pages. Some pages are composites of two or three photographs, so a page is not necessarily a single photograph or an independently titled work. Page four points to an image named dirt17; the web ordering cannot be assumed to be the performance chronology.

The artist describes a walk in Reposaari, taking a cup of dirt from one pile and releasing it at the next, ending in a burial ground. Individual photographs generally document static sites, cup states and contrasting patches rather than the action itself. A color contrast in a pile does not independently prove that the artist deposited it. Site-by-site image audits are registered in criticalDetailBatch22 and subsequent batches.
