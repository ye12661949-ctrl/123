# Walead Beshty — FedEx Glass Works (2007–)

Status: source-checked production research. This document distinguishes artwork from documentation photographs.

## Production chain

1. Make a laminated clear or two-way mirrored glass box matching a standardized FedEx shipping carton. The glass starts uncracked; lamination preserves structural integrity when cracks form.
2. Send the glass in its designated original FedEx box. Transit and handling cause cracks; the cracks are not pre-designed graphics.
3. Keep the cardboard box, accumulated shipping/tracking labels, customs forms, and repair tape. The box may be reinforced with a simple steel skeleton and reused as an exhibition support.
4. On each later exhibition, ship via FedEx in the same original box and orientation. Document each arrival photographically; add new shipping information to the artwork's expanding title.
5. Exhibit glass and original box together. There is no single fixed installation layout.

## Object studies

**2007 Large Box, Los Angeles–New York (Nov 27–28)** — Phillips lot 113 shows a tall transparent glass container on a flat white FedEx Express box; long fractures converge near the base. Materials: laminated glass, silicone, metal, carton, tape and labels. Glass 8.3 × 31.1 × 45.1 cm; carton 8.9 × 31.8 × 45.7 cm. Its shape derives from a shipping standard and its cracks from transit. https://www.phillips.com/detail/walead-beshty/79191

**2012 FedEx 10kg Box** — KADIST photograph shows a wider clear glass container resting on a brown multilingual 10kg carton; large radial fractures are not clearly visible in that photo. KADIST lists laminated glass, original carton and accumulated labels, but not the dimensions or detailed route. Do not infer that this is an earlier state of the 2007 object. https://kadist.org/work/fedex-10kg-box/

**Studio's 2009 Large Kraft Box example** — The artist's procedural record specifies laminated Mirropane, original carton, labels, silicone, metal, tape and 24 × 24 × 24 in packed dimensions. This dimension must not be assigned to either of the two objects above.
